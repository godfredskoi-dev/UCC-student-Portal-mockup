Financial Aid (Student Loan Trust Fund)

The Student Loan Trust Fund(SLTF) was established in December 2005 under the Trustee Incorporation ACT 1962, ACT 820

The objectives of the Trust Fund are to provide financial resourrces for the sound management of the Trust for the benefit of students and to help promote and facilitatethe national ideas enshrined in Article 28 and 38 of the 1992 constitution.

Students who want to apply for the financial aid should visit the following links below to know more about the Trust Fund and it's application process
